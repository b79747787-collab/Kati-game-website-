How to Play

03

Jump over an opponent's Kati when the board and rules allow it.

04

Win

Capture all of your opponent's Katis or win according to the game's active rules.

PLAY FAIR

Core Rules

  • Each opponent has 9 Katis.
  • Katis are placed on the board's line crossing points.
  • A player gets 15 seconds for a move.
  • Legal jumps can cross multiple Katis when the required blank spaces exist.
  • A player who loses all Katis loses the game.
  • Game and tournament settings can be updated by the developer.
